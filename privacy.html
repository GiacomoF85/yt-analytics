Privacy Policy — YT Analytics

YT Analytics is a personal, read-only tool that uses YouTube API Services to analyse YouTube channels authorised by their owner.

Data accessed

The tool reads channel and video metadata and performance statistics, including views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscriber changes. It does not upload, modify or delete videos.

Storage and use

OAuth credentials and exported reports are stored on the owner's computer. When the owner requests AI-assisted analysis, relevant report data may be processed by their chosen AI and messaging services, under those services' privacy policies. Data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can delete locally stored credentials and reports, and manage saved copies in their AI and messaging services. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Revoking access stops future access but does not automatically delete previously saved copies.

This informational website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not display channel statistics or OAuth credentials. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or application cookies. GitHub may process access logs and technical information as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
