YT Analytics

A private, read-only tool for analysing the owner's YouTube channel statistics.

Purpose

YT Analytics uses YouTube API Services to help its owner understand the performance of their own authorised channels and videos. It is a personal tool, not a public analytics service.

Read-only access

With the owner's Google authorisation, the tool reads channel and video metadata and performance statistics, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscriber changes. This information is used to prepare channel-performance reports and requested analyses.

The connector requests only the yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly permissions. It does not upload, edit or delete videos.

Privacy and control

Read the Privacy Policy for information about data access, storage, processing and revocation. By using this tool, the owner agrees to that policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
